# 11. Neuro-Academic Bridge

## Concept
A dedicated tool for students to calculate weighted averages based on academic coefficients. It solves the complexity of predicting final results in systems like the Baccalaureate, where subjects have different levels of importance.

## Innovation
The project implements **Dynamic Weighted Calculation**. Instead of a simple average, it processes a list of tuples (Grade, Coefficient) to provide a mathematically accurate representation of a student's standing.

## How to Run
1. Run: `python main.py`
2. Enter each subject name, your grade, and its coefficient.
3. Type 'done' to see your final weighted average and success status.
